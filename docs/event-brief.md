## Problem Statement

### Problem A — Disaster Response
During floods, heatwaves and other disasters, warnings may not reach everyone, while rescue teams, vehicles, shelters and medical supplies remain limited.

**Core question:** How can technology identify who needs help most and coordinate resources to reach them before the situation becomes critical?

> **Rules note:** Teams must choose **exactly 1** of the 3 problem domains.

## About the Hackathon

> **Build what matters**

Some of the best software begins with a simple idea, a sharp mind, and the ability to turn vision into execution.

The Vibe Coding Hackathon is built around exactly that. Hosted by the Department of Computer Science, UBIT, University of Karachi, as a flagship segment of Career Connect, it brings together students, creativity, AI-assisted development, and real-world problem-solving in a competitive environment designed around how modern software is built today.

Developers and technology enthusiasts from universities across the region transform ideas into functional digital products within a defined timeframe. Participants think beyond conventional coding, using AI-assisted tools, creative thinking, technical judgment, and rapid development to build solutions that are practical, purposeful, and impactful.

As part of Career Connect, the initiative extends beyond the hackathon by connecting students with professionals, organizations, and industry leaders. It creates opportunities for career exploration, mentorship, networking, and exposure to the evolving demands of the technology industry, helping students understand not only how to build, but where their skills can take them.

## Rules & Eligibility

- Teams of **2–4 members** including the lead.
- **University students only.** Bring your ID onsite.
- Choose **exactly 1** of the 3 problem domains.
- Submit a **live link** plus a **public GitHub repo** before time ends.
- **AI tools are allowed.** Pre-built or plagiarised projects are disqualified.
- **Late submissions are not evaluated.**
